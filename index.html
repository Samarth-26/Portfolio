about

Making raw data say something useful

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student with a growing focus on Data Analytics and Business Intelligence. I enjoy the full path from raw numbers to a usable answer — cleaning messy data, exploring it, visualizing it, and building dashboards or models that a non-technical reader can actually act on.

So far that's meant building a Retail Analytics Dashboard for customer and sales insights, a Phishing URL Detection System to flag malicious links, and a Fake News Detection System using NLP — three different problems, same underlying habit of turning data into a decision.

I'm continuously building depth in SQL, Tableau, Machine Learning, and Business Intelligence, and I'm always looking for the next dataset worth digging into.
